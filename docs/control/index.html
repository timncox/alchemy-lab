Control

Your module, on screen.

Turn every knob and setting from the browser, see what each one does, and save, load and back up presets. It talks to a module over USB, or to the emulator if you want to try it first.

USB needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. Plug the module's front USB-C into the computer, and on Tim's firmwares set USB port to Mac in Setup (the default).

Knobs and settings

Drag a dial up or down, use the arrow keys, or scroll on it; double-click for its default. Moving a knob on the module shows up here too. Changes are live but not saved: save them to a preset below.

Chord progression

Belt sings held notes from somewhere else right now (). This switches Settings, Sources, Chords from to Seq.
Pick each chord's scale degree, and tap its length to step 1, 2, 4, 8 bars. Same as the Launchpad's CHORDS page, and saved with a preset the same way.

Presets

Pick a slot.
Back up the sound you have now as a file, or bring one back.

Slot 1 is the Preset Slot knob turned all the way left, slot 16 all the way right.
